Hi, I'm Boshra

A Cairo-based modest fashion model — and an interior design student who believes elegance and modesty belong in the same frame.

Modeling, for me, is representing what modesty means. Every campaign is a chance to show that hijab is not a limit on style — it is the style. I've shot abayas, hijabs and modest ready-to-wear for thirteen brands, in studios, gardens and golden hour streets.

Off set, you'll find me sketching floor plans — turning raw layouts into vibrant homes.
