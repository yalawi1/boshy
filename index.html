01

Salam

Modeling, for me, is representing what modesty means. Every campaign is a chance to show that hijab is not a limit on style. It is the style.

I'm a Cairo based modest fashion model and an interior design student, and I believe elegance and modesty belong in the same frame. I've shot abayas, hijabs and modest ready to wear for thirteen brands, in studios, gardens and golden hour streets.

Off set, you'll find me sketching floor plans, turning raw layouts into vibrant homes.
