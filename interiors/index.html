Raheeq رحيق

Juice bar in an Islamic idiom · Commercial establishments

Retail

A shopfront built from mashrabiya geometry and a pointed arch. Inside, the zoning runs entrance to counter to pick up, with the fridges and showroom held on the flanks and a star tile motif set into the floor.
